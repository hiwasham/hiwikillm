# Andrej Karpathy homepage

> Andrej Karpathy’s personal homepage summarizes his AI research career, teaching, talks, writing, projects, and publications.

## TL;DR

- Karpathy describes himself as someone who likes “to train deep neural nets on large datasets” and is currently creating AI educational videos on YouTube in two tracks: technical and general-audience.
- 2023–2024: returned to [[OpenAI]] to build a team focused on [[midtraining]] and [[synthetic data generation]].
- 2017–2022: served as Director of AI at [[Tesla]], leading the [[Tesla Autopilot]] computer vision team and briefly [[Tesla Optimus]]; team handled in-house data labeling, neural network training, and deployment on Tesla’s custom inference chip.
- 2015–2017: research scientist and founding member at [[OpenAI]].
- PhD at [[Stanford Vision Lab]] under [[Fei-Fei Li]], focused on convolutional/recurrent neural networks for computer vision, NLP, and their intersection; designed and taught Stanford’s first deep learning course, [[CS 231n]].
- Major projects include [[micrograd]], [[char-rnn]], [[arxiv-sanity]], [[neuraltalk2]], [[ConvNetJS]], and [[ulogme]].
- Publications span image captioning, visual-semantic alignments, ImageNet, video classification, PixelCNN++, web-based agents, and simulated motor skills.

- Pet projects:
  - [[micrograd]]: “a tiny scalar-valued autograd engine”; implements backpropagation / reverse-mode autodiff over a dynamically built DAG, plus a small neural-network library with a PyTorch-like API.
  - [[char-rnn]]: Torch character-level language model using LSTMs/GRUs/RNNs.
  - [[arxiv-sanity]]: tool to manage Arxiv paper overload; supports discovery, search/sort by similarity, recent/popular papers, and recommendations; deployed at arxiv-sanity.com.
  - Related meta-research projects: [[pretty NIPS 2020 papers]], [[research lei]], [[scholaroctopus]], [[biomed-sanity]], and [[arxiv-sanity-lite]].
  - [[neuraltalk2]]: early image captioning project in Lua Torch; later extended with [[Justin Johnson]] to dense captioning.
  - [[ConvNetJS]]: deep learning library written from scratch in JavaScript, enabling browser-based convolutional-network demos.
  - Follow-ons: [[tSNEJS]], [[REINFORCEjs]], [[recurrentjs]], [[GANs in JS]].
  - [[ulogme]]: privacy-first productivity tracker written because he disliked RescueTime uploading private computer-usage statistics to the cloud.
